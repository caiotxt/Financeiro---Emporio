
## Project Overview

This project provides an analytical view of financial data from a company I work for, with the goal of generating insights and perspectives to support business growth and decision-making.

The analyses focus on understanding financial performance, cost structure, margins, and operational efficiency using data-driven approaches.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze financial data to understand revenue, costs, margins, and profitability  
- Support strategic and operational decision-making through data insights  
- Identify opportunities for efficiency improvements and business growth  
- Build a structured foundation for dashboards and future automation

---

## Results and Insights

- The project delivers insights such as:
- Financial performance trends over time
- Cost and margin analysis
- Identification of inefficiencies and optimization opportunities
- Strategic perspectives for sustainable business growth
